# The Science of Human Thinking → The Persona Decision Engine
### An eight-literature synthesis that rebuilds the persona's mind on the brain's real machinery

This is the foundational layer. Eight bodies of work — dual-process & bias, neuroeconomics, the predictive brain, affective neuroscience, memory & the narrative self, moral & social cognition, behavioral economics, and cross-cultural cognition — were read deep. The headline: **they are not eight theories to choose between. They nest into one architecture.** A real human decision is the brain minimizing prediction error, running a valuation engine, gated by a dual-process controller, colored by an interoceptive emotion system, drawing on a reconstructive memory, filtered through intuition-first morality, warped by scarcity, and — critically — parameterized by culture. Below is that unified engine, the per-persona knobs, an upgraded decision trace, and the honest caveats.

---

## The unifying insight

The deepest framework (predictive processing / active inference) **subsumes** several of the others, which is why the architecture is nested, not a list:
- **Belief** = a prior probability distribution held at some **precision**; **perception/learning** = updating it by prediction error. → Sticky priors, confirmation bias, and misinformation resistance fall out of *one* mechanism (high-precision identity-linked priors down-weight contradicting evidence).
- **Attention** = precision-weighting of prediction errors.
- **Emotion** = interoceptive inference — predictions about the body's state (Seth, Barrett). → ties affect directly into the same engine.
- **Action** = acting to make predictions come true (self-evidencing).

Onto that backbone bolt the specialized systems. Honest caveat up front: the Free Energy Principle is criticized as over-broad/unfalsifiable (Bowers & Davis) — so we use it as an **architectural scaffold to calibrate empirically**, not as an oracle.

## The unified decision engine (module by module)

### 1. Predictive backbone — *the persona is a prediction-error minimizer*
**Science:** Rao & Ballard (predictive coding); Friston (active inference); Clark/Hohwy; Seth/Barrett (interoception).
**Mechanism:** each persona holds beliefs as distributions with a **precision** parameter. Incoming evidence updates beliefs in proportion to *relative* precision. High-precision, identity-anchored priors resist updating — yielding realistic stubbornness and motivated reasoning as emergent behavior, not bespoke rules. **Curiosity** = a drive to reduce uncertainty (expected information gain), tunable per persona on an incurious↔inquisitive axis.
**India setting:** WhatsApp-forward belief adoption = precision contest between identity-aligned priors (high) and the new claim, weighted by source trust — the principled basis for vernacular-misinformation susceptibility.

### 2. Valuation & choice — *the brain's actual decision circuitry*
**Science:** Glimcher (common neural currency, vmPFC); Schultz (dopamine = reward-prediction-error, = TD learning; codes *marginal utility*); Berridge (**wanting ≠ liking**); Ratcliff (drift-diffusion); Damasio (somatic markers, Iowa Gambling Task).
**Mechanism:**
- Project every option onto a single **subjective-value latent** with a **concave** (diminishing-marginal) utility curve.
- Update values by **reward-prediction-error** (TD): surprise, disappointment, anticipation, and habit emerge for free.
- Keep **wanting (incentive salience)** and **liking (hedonic pleasure)** as *two decoupled state variables* — the only principled way to model craving, aspiration, and addiction (a persona can crave what it no longer enjoys: nicotine, status).
- Run the choice through a **drift-diffusion accumulator**: drift = value difference, **boundary = caution** (lowers under time pressure/fatigue → faster, sloppier), output = choice **+ reaction time + confidence**.
- A **somatic-marker** pre-filter emotionally tags options and biases the accumulator's starting point *before* deliberation ("something felt off").
**India setting:** very high tobacco prevalence + documented cue-reactivity → cue-triggered *wanting* spikes; aspirational consumption = incentive-salience gain on status cues, distinct from liking.

### 3. Dual-process controller — *fast by default, slow when it must be*
**Science:** Kahneman/Tversky; Gigerenzer (ecological rationality, heuristics as competence); Simon (bounded rationality, satisficing); Stanovich (reflective disposition); Daw/Dolan (model-free vs model-based RL); De Neys/Melnikoff (it's *not* two literal systems).
**Mechanism:** one pipeline with a **deliberation gate**. Default = fast associative pass (attribute substitution: representativeness, availability, anchoring; satisficing to an aspiration level). A **conflict/stakes detector** decides whether to spend "System 2" compute. Gate opens on: stakes × time × *inverse* fatigue × *inverse* scarcity × a per-persona **reflective-disposition** knob. Action control **arbitrates model-free (habit, cached, not re-evaluated) vs model-based (deliberate)**; **stress/load/fatigue → fall back to habit.** Treat heuristics as *ecologically rational*, not as errors to remove — most real decisions are fast, and over-deliberating personas are *less* human.
**Honest caveat:** "two systems" is a useful abstraction, not neuroanatomy; loss-aversion λ is *not* a universal constant (see §7).

### 4. Emotion engine — *two layers + a culture pack*
**Science:** LeDoux (response vs feeling split); Barrett (constructed emotion, core affect = valence×arousal); Panksepp (7 primary drives: SEEKING, FEAR, RAGE, CARE, PANIC/GRIEF, PLAY, LUST); Scherer/OCC (appraisal); affect-as-information / mood-congruence.
**Mechanism (hybrid — don't pick one camp):**
- **Layer 1 — Core affect:** an always-on valence×arousal state that drifts, decays to baseline, and carries **incidental mood** (fatigue, prior events) that biases the *next* choice.
- **Layer 2 — Motivational primitives:** Panksepp drives as biological, cross-cultural baselines that push affect and gate behavior.
- **Appraisal glue:** event → checks (goal-relevance, conduciveness, coping potential, norm-compatibility) → update affect + fire drives (OCC/EMA implementable).
- **Concept/label layer:** the discrete emotion *word* is applied last, by **culturally-learned concepts** — a **swappable culture pack**.
- Emotion then biases **attention** (threat capture), **memory** (mood-congruent retrieval), and **risk** (fear → risk-averse; anger → risk-seeking).
**India setting:** keep Layers 1–2 culture-invariant; make appraisal weights + emotion vocabulary an **Indian pack** — e.g. **lajja** (not Western "shame": socially ubiquitous, relational, gendered, *positively* valued as propriety), and relational appraisal priorities over individual-goal conduciveness.

### 5. Memory — *reconstructive, drifting, narrated — never a log*
**Science:** Tulving (episodic vs semantic, mental time travel); Bartlett (schema assimilation/leveling/sharpening); Schacter (seven sins); Loftus (false memories, ~25–50%); Nader/LeDoux (**reconsolidation: recall = re-write**); Graybiel (habit chunking); Gazzaniga (left-hemisphere **interpreter** confabulates reasons); Conway (working-self biases retrieval); Kahneman (**peak-end rule, duration neglect**).
**Mechanism:** two stores (stable semantic facts vs re-simulated first-person episodic scenes). Memories **level + sharpen toward the persona's schemas**; old negatives soften; retrieval is mood-gated; trusted suggestion can implant events. **Every recall re-encodes** (drifts toward current context/emotion). Episodes compress to **peak + end** snapshots with duration neglect. A **working-self goal stack** biases retrieval to identity-congruent memories; the **interpreter** manufactures confident post-hoc rationalizations — *comfortable being wrong about its own causes.*
**India setting:** a few **ritually-rehearsed, non-lossy** memories (prayers, family sayings, songs — Vedic oral tradition) resist the decay applied to ordinary episodes; much autobiographical memory is **socially co-constructed** across kin (events "remembered" only because the family retells them).

### 6. Moral & social cognition — *intuition first, reasons after, permeable to others*
**Science:** Haidt (social intuitionism, the elephant & rider, Moral Foundations); Greene (dual-process morality — emotional deontology vs effortful utilitarianism; vmPFC patients go utilitarian); Festinger (dissonance reduction); Cialdini (7 levers: reciprocity, commitment, social proof, authority, liking, scarcity, **unity**); Asch (~75% conform at least once) / Milgram (65% to max) / Tajfel (minimal-group in-group favoritism); Tomasello (cooperative shared intentionality); Shweder (Big Three: Autonomy/**Community**/**Divinity**).
**Mechanism:** moral judgment = a **fast affective verdict** (from Moral-Foundations weights + Greene alarms); reasoning is **post-hoc justification** (the §5 interpreter again), rarely reversing the gut alone — reversal needs effortful "manual mode" and surfaces as conflict, and happens more via *social* argument than solitary thought. Acting against a value under weak pressure → **dissonance reduction** (revise the value / rationalize). Behavior is **permeable to Cialdini levers and social proof**, rising under uncertainty/load; **in-group/identity weighted** heavily; authority deferred to (modulated by proximity & dissenting allies).
**India setting:** weight **Community + Divinity** as first-class moral concerns (role-duty, hierarchy, purity, karmic order); relational duty can *outrank* abstract justice (Miller & Bersoff); **honor/shame (izzat, lajja)** — collective, attaching to family — is the primary regulatory emotion; caste/community sets a birth-ascribed in/out-group; high deference (Hofstede PDI ~77).

### 7. Economic cognition — *prospect-theoretic, present-biased, scarcity-bent*
**Science:** Kahneman/Tversky prospect theory (reference dependence; λ≈2.25, α≈0.88 — *contested as non-constant*); Laibson (quasi-hyperbolic **β-δ** present bias → commitment devices); Loewenstein (**visceral factors, hot-cold empathy gap**, projection bias); Thaler (**mental accounting**, endowment, nudges); Ariely (arbitrary coherence/anchoring, zero-price, social vs market norms); Mullainathan & Shafir (**Scarcity**: tunneling + bandwidth tax — the Indian **sugarcane-farmer study, ~13 IQ-point reversible deficit**).
**Mechanism:** evaluate choices as **gains/losses vs a (recent-income) reference point**; **mental-account buckets** are non-fungible (food vs festival vs remittance, spent by different rules); **present-bias β collapses** under acute scarcity or visceral states; **scarcity** narrows planning horizon and injects noise (bandwidth tax), tunnels onto the immediate need, neglects insurance/fine-print — and is **reversible** (restores at payday/harvest). Make `β`, planning depth, decision noise, and money-domain risk-aversion **functions of a continuous scarcity-state variable**, not fixed traits. This single move is what separates a real low-income Indian persona from a generic "irrational agent."

### 8. The culture pack — *the master correction that makes it Indian*
**Science:** Nisbett (holistic/dialectical vs analytic — South Asia leans holistic, *madhyam marg*); Henrich (**WEIRD**: Western samples are outliers; **LLMs are hyper-WEIRD** and default to individualist/self-expression values regardless of prompt language); Markus & Kitayama (**interdependent self**); Shweder (Big Three); Miller (**attribution is situational, not dispositional, in India** — the FAE is a WEIRD acquisition); Sinha (karma/dharma + **secondary control**).
**This is not a module — it reweights every module above.** The standing India parameters:
- **Attribution → situational/relational** (suppress the model's reflexive dispositionalism).
- **Self-construal → interdependent** (identity = family/jati/role/duty before individual preference).
- **Cognition → holistic/dialectical** (attend to the field; tolerate contradiction; seek the middle path, not a single logical winner).
- **Morality → all Big Three**, Community + Divinity weighted.
- **Hierarchy → high power distance** (age/status-graded register).
- **Locus → karmic/external + pragmatic effort** coexisting.
- **Counter-measure:** because base LLMs are hyper-WEIRD, this requires **sustained explicit conditioning held as standing constraints** — a one-line "you are Indian" label is not enough — *while* preserving real internal variance (caste/region/religion/urban-rural/generation), never collapsing into a monolithic "Indian."

## The per-persona knob vector (what actually varies)

Each persona = the demographic skeleton + this parameter set, sampled sub-nationally and partly **state-dependent** (changes hour to hour / season to season):

| Knob | Range | Driven by |
|---|---|---|
| prior_precision (per belief) | low→high | identity-centrality; stubbornness |
| reflective_disposition | low→high | education, numeracy, need-for-cognition |
| utility curvature α; loss-aversion λ; prob-weighting γ | distributions | domain, stakes (λ context-conditioned) |
| present-bias β; long-run δ | β collapses under scarcity | income, scarcity_state, visceral state |
| **scarcity_state** | continuous | days-to-payday, cash-vs-subsistence, harvest cycle |
| drive baselines (SEEKING/FEAR/CARE/…) | per-persona | temperament |
| wanting / liking (per reward) | decoupled | addiction/aspiration history |
| moral-foundation / Big-Three weights | vector | community, religiosity, region |
| power_distance · self_construal · attribution_style · cognition_style · locus | India-set defaults, varied | sub-national culture sampling |
| emotion-concept pack | culture pack | language/community (lajja, etc.) |
| somatic-marker associations | learned tags | autobiographical history |

## Upgraded decision trace (Sunita, ₹99/month micro-insurance)

1. **Predictive backbone:** the pitch violates a high-precision prior ("phone-money disappears; outsiders want commission") → the claim is down-weighted before it's even evaluated.
2. **Somatic marker + core affect:** a fast avoid-tag fires (dengue-debt memory association); she's on a Teej **fast** → low arousal/patience, **drift-diffusion boundary low** → she'll decide fast and curtly.
3. **Valuation:** ₹99/month is a recurring **loss** vs her reference point; **mental accounting** flags it would raid the *committed* school-fund bucket; **present-bias β** (elevated by remittance-late **scarcity**) discounts the future protection.
4. **Dual-process:** stakes moderate, fatigue high → gate stays mostly **fast/habitual**; she satisfices to "not now."
5. **Moral/social:** intuition-first caution + **social proof / unity** — she'll trust it only if the SHG (in-group, Rekha-didi = authority+liking) vets it; **face**: "what will the samiti say if I'm cheated?"
6. **Culture pack:** **interdependent self** → defers to husband (power distance); **karmic locus** → "Bhagwan rakhega"; **situational** framing of the agent's motive.
7. **Interpreter** narrates a coherent reason ("*pahile aadmi se, phir samiti se puchhungi*") — she is unaware the real drivers were a somatic tag + a fasting body + a precision-weighted prior.

Same surface decision as before (defer), but now it is *produced by the actual machinery* — which means it generalizes correctly to new scenarios and is falsifiable parameter-by-parameter.

## Honest caveats (carry these into the build)
- **Loss aversion** λ≈2.25 is **not** a constant (Gal & Rucker; small losses ≈ neutral) — treat λ as a context-conditioned distribution.
- **Dual-process "two systems"** is an abstraction, not literal architecture (Melnikoff & Bargh; De Neys) — implement one pipeline with a gate.
- **Free Energy Principle** is criticized as unfalsifiable/over-broad — scaffold, then calibrate.
- **Basic vs constructed emotion** is unsettled — hence the hybrid (drives + core affect + cultural concepts).
- **Reconsolidation** clinical effects (propranolol) are mixed — use the *mechanism* (recall = rewrite), not strong claims.
- **Hofstede** scores have methodological limits; **never flatten India** into one culture score (Ortner).
